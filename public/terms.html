What the hosted Single Run is

A one-time, automated, URL-only audit of up to 8 public pages of the site you name, run from our servers with no browser session, no login and no access to your code. It produces a 0 to 100 score and a list of findings on a private page tied to your Stripe checkout session. That page is the deliverable. We do not send the report by email. The link is a bearer link: anyone who has it can open the report, so treat it like a receipt.
